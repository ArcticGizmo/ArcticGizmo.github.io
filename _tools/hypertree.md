4D chess of virtual desktops. Give each task its own branch — a named stack of
Windows desktops hanging below an anchor — then dive in and surface back out
with a single keystroke.

Instead of one flat row of desktops, Hypertree lets you nest context so a deep
dive never loses your place in the bigger picture.
