A snappy, tray-resident replacement for the Windows Snipping Tool. Grab a
region, window, or monitor with a hotkey, then annotate with arrows, boxes,
highlights, text and numbered badges — or irreversibly redact anything
sensitive before you share it.

Record your screen, trim the clip, and export with format presets (Slack-small,
GIF, WebP), all without leaving the tray.
